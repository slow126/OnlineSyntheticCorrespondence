## 1. What the paper is (one paragraph, for orientation)

Thesis: which synthetic training set transfers best is regime-conditional. In BFV
motion space, d_T→B = off-target mass (precision-like), d_B→T = missing support
(recall-like). From-scratch models pay both costs (utility tracks the sum = symmetric
average); a pretrained backbone forgives off-target mass, leaving missing support as
the sole cost. Because the two distances are anti-correlated across natural sources,
averaging the forgiven cost back in corrupts pretrained rankings — which is why the
original ECCV "directed coverage" looked washed out. Evidence: 99 models / 891
evaluations (3 archs x 9 variants x 11 sources x 9 benchmarks, Middlebury excluded),
flip in 8/9 variants (exact p=0.0286), DINO null, fit-free regime policy at
rho≈0.50–0.52 ≈ reproducibility ceiling, anchors+policy MAE 8–9 PCK, and a pre-registered
OOS pass on the intervention grid's scratch arm (FlyingThings rho=+0.67 where off-target
varies 3.1x). Limitations candidly state the pretrained half is observational.
