Table~\ref{tab:law} and Fig.~\ref{fig:coverage_law_a} present the core finding.
\emph{Coverage} ($\dbt$) is the consistent positive predictor, up to $+0.73$ on
real motion and on semantic targets too (CATs++ $+0.50$, GLU-Net $+0.80$). It is the best
predictor in every backbone real-motion cell and stays positive on semantic targets
($+0.31$ to $+0.80$); only end-to-end RAFT ties a symmetric distance, and there the
parity is the recall that distance shares with coverage, not its off-target term. On semantic targets coverage no longer dominates: a
symmetric or sliced-$W_2$ distance is competitive there, and strongest for
FlowFormer (Table~\ref{tab:law}), since semantic matching also rewards the
full-distribution compatibility those distances partly carry. Across the whole
table coverage remains the reliable default: positive in every cell,
best-or-tied in the large majority, and the one distance worth reaching for without
knowing the configuration, whereas off-target mass ($\dtb$) is erratic and near-zero on
its own. The source
ranking this produces for every target, set against realized transfer, is laid
out in Fig.~\ref{fig:consensus} below. In DINOv3 appearance space the identical
analysis is null throughout (Table~\ref{tab:lawdino}): the target-selective signal
lives in motion, not in how the frames look.


\noindent\textbf{Robustness.} Coverage's lead replicates across distance
estimators and generator families, and is not explained by dataset size (ranking
by \#samples or \#vectors reaches only $\rho\le0.27$). Jackknifing coverage's
correlation---leaving out each training source ($\times 11$) and each
generator family ($\times 5$) in turn---holds it within
$[{+}0.57,{+}0.65]$, with no single family load-bearing. The directed distances
are also sampling-stable, reaching $\rho\ge0.96$ by $5{\times}10^4$ flow
vectors and $1.000$ at our reference budget, whereas density-based (KL) estimators
never converge---motivating our restriction to support-geometry estimators.
FlowFormer entered the
study as a held-out fourth architecture after the analysis was frozen and
reproduces the coverage signal on real-motion targets without adjustment
(Table~\ref{tab:law}), a check that the analysis was not tuned to the data.
The supplement gives the full per-configuration tables and all ablations: the two
alternative estimators ($\epsilon$-radius coverage and a density kNN-KL),
generator and sampling controls, and the direction-blind pooling analysis.
