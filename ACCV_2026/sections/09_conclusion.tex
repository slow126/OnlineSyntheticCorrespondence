\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

A single directed motion distance is enough to choose training data. Ranking
candidate sources by how well their motion covers the target's recovers $92\%$ of
transfer's reproducibility ceiling ($\rho{=}0.57$), and its top pick surrenders only
$0.6$ \pck{} to the oracle best source, against $11.5$ for an appearance ranking and
$15.1$ for a random one. The coverage direction carries this signal on its own, the
off-target half is nearly inert, and appearance is target-agnostic, so the symmetric
distances that blend directions never lead. A few reference runs turn the ranking into
an absolute estimate accurate to $9.0$ \pck{}. Because the objective reads off motion
geometry with nothing rendered and no model trained, it also \emph{designs} data, and
the same coverage search steered two very different generators toward a target,
lifting SDF-Fractals3D's KITTI accuracy at the strict $1\%$ threshold from at most
$40$ to $89$. Before improving how a source renders, measure how well its motion
covers the target's.
