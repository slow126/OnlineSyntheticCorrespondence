\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limits}

We state the coverage law's evidentiary status precisely. Reported with each
architecture in its designed regime (Sec.~\ref{sec:setup}), coverage is the
consistent positive predictor and survives every robustness control we ran:
estimator replication, leave-one-benchmark-out, self-pair exclusion, and a
generator-family jackknife (supplement). Training the backbone-dependent matchers
off-design (from scratch) mode-collapses them to a near-static prior; we report
those configurations in full in the supplement but do
not read them as a coverage result, since a model that ignores its input carries
no data-selection signal. That partition follows from architecture design, fixed
before any motion distance is measured, not from the transfer outcomes.

\noindent\emph{Semantic correspondence is the hardest case.} Even under a
backbone, motion descriptors capture coverage but not full semantic
compatibility, so a symmetric or sliced-$W_2$ distance is competitive there and,
for FlowFormer, better (Sec.~\ref{sec:law}); the target-selective signal still
lives in motion, but coverage is no longer the lone predictor.

\noindent\emph{The causal evidence is concentrated on a camera-motion target.}
Our interventional legs---render-free recovery of a target's motion regime, a
tuned source beating a generic baseline, and the inverted-U magnitude grid---are
strongest on KITTI, which is ego-motion-dominated; real-image \emph{motion}
transfer is carried mainly by KITTI in our benchmark set (Sec.~\ref{sec:setup}).
Object-centric targets are harder for an aggregate motion descriptor, which is by
construction blind to the camera-versus-object factorization of a flow field, and
extending the controlled interventions to them is the natural next step.

\noindent\emph{One controlled comparison remains open.} The magnitude grid shows
transfer is inverted-U in motion magnitude (coverage lifts it up to the match,
over-shoot lowers it), and a fixed-coverage off-target ladder shows that injecting
off-target motion barely moves transfer ($|\Delta|\le2.4$ \pck), bounding
off-target mass as a sensitivity to degenerate motion rather than a natural
co-equal cost. But magnitude moves coverage and off-target mass together, so a
clean test that varies coverage breadth at \emph{fixed} off-target mass remains
future work our render-free infrastructure supports.

\noindent\emph{Absolute accuracy beyond anchors is feature-unreachable} in our
analysis: coverage supplies the ranking, but the absolute \pck{} level must be
read off a few observed runs (Sec.~\ref{sec:absolute}).

One benchmark (Middlebury) was excluded after we identified an evaluation-harness
defect during analysis; all reported numbers use the remaining nine (the
supplement documents the exclusion and a sensitivity analysis).
